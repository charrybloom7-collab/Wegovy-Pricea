Why the Listed Price May Not Be Your Final Cost

Online medication prices can appear straightforward, but several details affect the amount a customer actually pays.

Formulation and Strength

The oral tablet and injectable pen have different pricing structures. Prices may also vary between strengths, particularly when a manufacturer offers special introductory pricing or a separate price for a higher-dose product.

A customer should compare the exact product prescribed by their healthcare professional. Selecting a product based only on its lower advertised price could result in comparing two different formulations or strengths.

Insurance Benefits and Deductibles

Insurance coverage can substantially affect the amount due at checkout. Some plans cover Wegovy for qualifying indications, while others may impose restrictions, require prior authorization, or exclude coverage.

A deductible may also affect out-of-pocket costs. Consequently, an advertised insurance price should be treated as a possible starting point rather than a guarantee. Customers should confirm their benefits with the insurer and pharmacy.

Savings Offers and Coupons

Manufacturer savings programs may reduce costs for eligible patients. However, each offer has its own rules, including restrictions on who can participate, how savings are applied, and whether other discounts can be combined with the offer.

Before relying on a coupon, check its current terms and confirm the amount that will actually be charged.
